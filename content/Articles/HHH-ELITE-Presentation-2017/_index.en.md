This comprehensive presentation document captures the essence of the strategic partnership between the House-Hotel-Home project and ELITE Interior magazine throughout 2017. The materials provide a complete overview of how these two influential organizations collaborated to advance the discourse on contemporary hospitality and residential design in Russia and beyond.

The presentation showcases the project's core concept, mission, and vision, detailing how HHH serves as a vital platform for connecting architects, designers, material suppliers, and hospitality industry professionals. It includes visual documentation of exhibition layouts, business program structures, and the innovative formats used to facilitate knowledge exchange and professional networking among participants.

Readers will find detailed information about the 2017 event calendar, featuring highlights from major exhibitions and conferences where the HHH-ELITE collaboration was presented. The document illustrates successful case studies, partnership models, and the impact of the collaboration on industry trends. It demonstrates how the partnership created value for all stakeholders—from designers seeking inspiration to manufacturers looking for market insights.

The presentation also outlines the media strategy, audience reach, and engagement metrics that made the 2017 collaboration a benchmark for future initiatives. With professional graphics, statistics, and testimonials from industry leaders, this document serves as both a retrospective of achievements and a roadmap for future developments in the hospitality design sector.

This bilingual presentation is an invaluable resource for anyone interested in understanding how strategic partnerships can drive innovation in the architecture and design industries, making it essential reading for business developers, marketing professionals, and industry decision-makers.
