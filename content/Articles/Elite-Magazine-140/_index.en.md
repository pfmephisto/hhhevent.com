This landmark issue of ELITE Interior magazine provides comprehensive coverage of the House-Hotel-Home project, featuring in-depth reports on multiple events, exhibitions, and the innovative partnerships that have shaped the hospitality design landscape in Russia. Issue 140 stands as a definitive documentation of HHH's impact on contemporary architecture and interior design, bringing together insights from industry leaders, project showcases, and forward-looking perspectives on the future of hospitality spaces.

The magazine features exclusive articles on key figures in the Russian architecture scene, including prominent architects and designers who have contributed to the HHH platform. Readers will find detailed coverage of major exhibitions such as Batimat Russia and Heimtextil, where the project demonstrated cutting-edge approaches to hotel and residential design. The issue also explores the strategic collaboration between HHH and ELITE Interior, illustrating how media partnerships can amplify industry discourse and drive innovation.

Through stunning photography, technical analysis, and expert commentary, Issue 140 presents a comprehensive view of the hospitality design ecosystem. The publication examines innovative materials and technologies, explores the work of international partners including German and Italian design firms, and provides case studies of successful implementations in hotel and apartment projects. Special attention is given to the role of leading manufacturers and suppliers in bringing visionary design concepts to reality.

This collector's edition serves as both a historical record of a pivotal moment in Russian hospitality design and a valuable reference for professionals seeking to understand the evolution of contemporary architecture and interior design in the sector. With contributions from renowned architects, designers, manufacturers, and industry analysts, Issue 140 is essential reading for anyone involved in hotel development, residential architecture, and commercial interior design, offering insights that continue to influence the industry's direction.
