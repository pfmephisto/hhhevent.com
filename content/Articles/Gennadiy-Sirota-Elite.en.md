This exclusive report features Gennadiy Sirota, one of Russia's most prominent architects and a key figure in shaping Moscow's modern skyline. As the Head of Studio No. 6 at "Mosproekt-2" Directorate and Chief Architect of the Moscow International Business Center (Moscow-City MIBC), Sirota has been instrumental in defining contemporary Russian architecture.

The article, published in ELITE Interior magazine, offers readers an intimate look into Sirota's design philosophy, his approach to large-scale urban projects, and his vision for the future of hospitality and residential design. Through detailed interviews and project showcases, the report explores how his work bridges international architectural trends with Russian cultural context.

Readers will discover insights into Sirota's involvement with the House-Hotel-Home project, where his expertise in creating functional yet aesthetically striking spaces has contributed significantly to the project's success. The article examines his innovative solutions for modern living and working environments, with particular focus on how architecture can enhance human experience.

The report includes stunning photography of Sirota's projects, technical drawings, and commentary from colleagues and clients, providing a comprehensive portrait of an architect who continues to push boundaries in contemporary design. This is essential reading for anyone interested in Russian architecture, urban development, and the evolution of hospitality design.
