This comprehensive report documents the groundbreaking collaboration between the House-Hotel-Home project and ELITE Interior magazine, one of Russia's leading publications in architecture and interior design. The partnership brought together experts, designers, and industry leaders to explore innovative approaches to hospitality and residential design.

The article provides detailed coverage of the joint exhibition, featuring exclusive interviews with participating architects and designers, insights into the latest trends in hotel and apartment design, and analysis of emerging materials and technologies. Readers will find in-depth discussions on how contemporary design principles are reshaping the hospitality industry.

Through stunning photography and expert commentary, this report captures the essence of the HHH-ELITE collaboration, highlighting successful case studies and innovative solutions that are setting new standards in the industry. The publication serves as both a documentation of the event and a valuable resource for professionals seeking inspiration and practical knowledge.

This bilingual report features contributions from renowned architects, interior designers, and industry specialists, making it an essential read for anyone interested in the intersection of hospitality design, residential architecture, and commercial interior solutions.
