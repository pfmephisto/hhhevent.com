The Hotel House Home project served as a partner of the business program at the Construct Crimea construction exhibition, which took place from October 25-27, 2018. This event became an important platform for exchanging experience and discussing prospects for the development of the tourism industry and hospitality construction in the region.

Leading experts from Russia and Germany participated in a round table discussion titled "Analysis of International Experience in Design and Construction of Tourism and Recreation Facilities." Speakers with extensive experience in designing and constructing tourism and recreation facilities shared insights on landscape development based on principles of ecological architecture, as well as construction using ecological materials according to international and Russian standards.

As part of the business program, leading designers presented modern construction technologies and materials, interior trends, and new collections of finishing materials and equipment. Participants had the opportunity to familiarize themselves with best practices in hotel design and furnishing, and to discuss current issues of sustainable development and ecological construction.

The participation of the Hotel House Home project in the Construct Crimea exhibition emphasized the importance of international cooperation in developing tourism infrastructure and demonstrated how knowledge exchange between Russian and foreign specialists contributes to improving the quality of hospitality projects and implementing innovative solutions in the construction and design of tourism and recreation facilities.
