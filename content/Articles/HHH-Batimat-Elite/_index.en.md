This comprehensive report documents the House-Hotel-Home project's successful participation at Batimat Russia 2018, one of the country's most prestigious architecture and construction exhibitions. Held from April 3-6, 2018 at Crocus Expo IEC in Moscow, this event marked a significant milestone in the project's evolution, introducing the groundbreaking theme "Apartments of the Future: Innovative Materials and Technologies."

The article, published in ELITE Interior magazine, provides extensive coverage of the exhibition's highlights, including the stunning model apartment designed by MSF Architecture (Germany), featuring multiple installations such as a bedroom, bathroom, and children's corner. The report captures the innovative use of materials and design solutions that set new standards for modern hospitality and residential spaces.

Readers will find detailed accounts of the intensive business program, including high-profile round tables with industry leaders such as Gennady Sirota, Dmitry Barkhin, Paola Vinciguerra, and Maria Sonne-Frederiksen. The article explores discussions on innovative materials, luxury apartment design, Italian style influences, and the future of urban landscape architecture. These conversations shaped the discourse on contemporary hospitality design in Russia.

The report also documents the educational component of the event, featuring presentations on lighting design, functional interior solutions, and the latest trends in building materials and technologies. Through exclusive photography, expert commentary, and visitor testimonials, this article captures the energy and innovation that characterized the HHH presence at Batimat 2018.

This publication serves as both a historical record of a landmark event and a valuable resource for architects, designers, and developers interested in the latest innovations in hospitality and residential design. It demonstrates how collaboration between international experts and Russian industry leaders is shaping the future of building and interior design.
