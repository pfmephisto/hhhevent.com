This feature article from ZAHOD magazine's issue 121 (2016) provides an in-depth exploration of the House-Hotel-Home project during its formative year, capturing the initiative's innovative vision and early achievements. ZAHOD, a respected publication focusing on architecture, design, and urban development, offered its readers unique insights into how HHH was establishing itself as a vital platform for the hospitality design community.

The article examines the conceptual foundation of the House-Hotel-Home project, exploring how it bridges the gap between residential comfort and hotel functionality. Through interviews with founders, participating architects, and industry partners, the report reveals the thinking behind the project's unique approach to creating dialogue between designers, manufacturers, and hospitality professionals. Readers will gain understanding of the challenges and opportunities in the Russian hospitality market circa 2016.

The publication documents early exhibitions and events, showcasing how the HHH platform quickly became a meeting point for innovation in hotel design, apartment concepts, and residential solutions. The article features project examples, material innovations, and design trends that were shaping the industry at the time, providing valuable historical context for understanding the project's evolution.

With expert analysis, striking photography, and forward-looking perspectives, this ZAHOD article captures a pivotal moment in the development of Russia's contemporary hospitality design scene. The report explores how international collaborations and cross-cultural exchanges were beginning to transform the domestic market, introducing new standards of quality and design thinking.

This publication serves as both a historical document of the HHH project's origins and a valuable resource for understanding how the Russian hospitality design industry has evolved over recent years, making it essential reading for design historians, industry analysts, and professionals interested in market development trends.
