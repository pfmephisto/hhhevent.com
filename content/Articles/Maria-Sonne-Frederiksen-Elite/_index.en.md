This feature article highlights the work and philosophy of Maria Sonne-Frederiksen, a distinguished German architect whose innovative approach to hospitality and residential design has earned international recognition. As the principal of MSF Architecture, a member of Architektenkammer BDA Hannover, and Professor at MAAM (Germany), Sonne-Frederiksen brings a unique European perspective to contemporary design challenges.

Published in ELITE Interior magazine, this report explores Sonne-Frederiksen's collaboration with the House-Hotel-Home project, where her expertise in creating sustainable, human-centered spaces has been instrumental. The article delves into her design methodology, which seamlessly blends functionality with aesthetic innovation, drawing on both traditional craftsmanship and cutting-edge technology.

Readers will gain insights into Sonne-Frederiksen's award-winning projects across Europe and Russia, examining how her cross-cultural approach enriches the dialogue between German precision and Russian creativity. The report features detailed case studies of hotel and residential projects that exemplify her commitment to quality, sustainability, and timeless design.

Through interviews, project photography, and technical analysis, this article provides a comprehensive look at an architect who is redefining standards in the hospitality industry. Her work demonstrates how thoughtful design can create spaces that are not only beautiful but also enhance the well-being of their occupants, making this essential reading for design professionals and enthusiasts alike.
